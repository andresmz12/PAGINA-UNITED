Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Who we are

United Cleaning Services ("United Cleaning", "we", "us") provides commercial and residential cleaning services in Missouri, Kansas, and Southern Illinois. This policy explains what information we collect through unitedcleaning.us, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

When you request a quote or contact us, you may give us your name, email address, phone number, company name, property type, the service you need, and any details you include in your message. If you call, email, or message us on WhatsApp, we receive the information you share in that conversation. Our servers and hosting provider may also log basic technical data such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your request, prepare quotes, schedule and perform services, communicate with you about your account, and improve our website and services. We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the website and handle requests — for example, our website hosting provider and the form-handling service that delivers your message to our email — and when required by law. These providers may process data only to perform services for us.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not currently use advertising cookies. If we add analytics or advertising tools in the future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent.

Retention and security

We keep your information only as long as needed to respond to you, provide services, and meet legal or accounting obligations. We use reasonable measures to protect it, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices and rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, and you may opt out of marketing messages at any time. Depending on where you live (for example, California or other states with privacy laws), you may have additional rights. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Children

Our website is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.

Contact us

Questions or requests: Info@unitedcleaning.us or (800) 555-0142.
